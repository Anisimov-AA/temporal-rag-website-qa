Yes. Dupaco offers private student loans through its partner CU Student Choice. The Extra Credit Student Loan covers gap costs federal aid leaves behind (tuition, room and board, textbooks, lab fees). You apply once and can secure funding for your entire undergraduate career without reapplying each year. Repayment is flexible: you can defer principal and interest payments while enrolled, and choose from flexible terms, including graduated repayment after graduation.
